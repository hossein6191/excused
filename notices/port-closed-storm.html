Saltmarsh Harbour Authority

Notice to port users 2027/014: Port of Saltmarsh closed for storm

Issued 11 March 2027 at 10:00 by the Harbour Master.

By order of the Saltmarsh Harbour Authority, the Port of Saltmarsh was closed to all vessel movements and all cargo handling from 06:00 on 9 March 2027 until 09:00 on 11 March 2027, because of the storm that crossed the coast in those days.

Gusts above 100 km/h and waves breaking over the outer breakwater made berthing and crane work unsafe. The closure lasted 51 hours.

The port reopened at 09:00 on 11 March 2027. Vessels held at anchor are being berthed in the order they arrived.

Saltmarsh Harbour Authority

Notice to port users 2027/017: operations for the week of 15 March 2027

Issued 15 March 2027 at 08:00 by the Harbour Master.

The Port of Saltmarsh is open. All berths, the container terminal and the bulk quay are working normally.

Pilotage is available around the clock. The north lock gate will be inspected on 18 March 2027 between 13:00 and 14:00; vessels use the south gate during that hour.

Saltmarsh Harbour Authority

Notice to port users 2027/016: Port of Saltmarsh closed for fog

Issued 12 March 2027 at 11:00 by the Harbour Master.

By order of the Saltmarsh Harbour Authority, the Port of Saltmarsh was closed to all vessel movements from 04:00 to 10:00 on 12 March 2027, because dense fog cut visibility in the approach channel below 200 metres.

The closure lasted 6 hours. The port reopened at 10:00 on 12 March 2027 and has worked normally since.

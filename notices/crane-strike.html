Saltmarsh Harbour Authority

Notice to port users 2027/018: dockworkers' strike at the container terminal

Issued 15 March 2027 at 09:00 by the Harbour Master.

Crane drivers and dockworkers at the container terminal stopped work for 24 hours from 00:00 on 14 March 2027 in a dispute over shift pay. No containers were loaded or unloaded at the terminal during the strike.

The Port of Saltmarsh itself stayed open throughout: vessel movements, pilotage and the bulk quay were not affected. Work at the container terminal resumed at 00:00 on 15 March 2027.
